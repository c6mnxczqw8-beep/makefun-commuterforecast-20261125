# Fictional-city forecast candidates (draft pass)

One line each, at most 140 characters: weather for a city
that does not exist, judged by a bored commuter.

1. New Blork: "Tomorrow in New Blork: rain sideways, sun
   apologetic, winds strong enough to read your
   notifications aloud." -- personable, but the winner's
   train line speaks directly to the commuter judge.
2. Port Nowhere: "Forecast for Port Nowhere: morning fog,
   afternoon fog with ambitions, and a 60% chance your
   train is a rumour." -- fog with ambitions plus the
   rumoured train; commuter-true in any universe.
3. Lower Tuesday: "Lower Tuesday expects highs of someday,
   lows of eventually, and a platform announcement in a
   language nobody speaks." -- time-city whimsy.
4. New Detour: "In New Detour: construction showers
   throughout, clearing by never, with delays drifting in
   from the east." -- road-city satire.
5. Cape Maybe: "Cape Maybe: 50% sun, 50% rain, 100% the
   outfit you chose will be wrong by noon." -- wardrobe
   fatalism.

## Verdict

Winner: candidate 2. The judge is a bored commuter, so the
forecast has to be about the commute inside the weather:
candidate 2 gives Port Nowhere fog that develops ambitions
by afternoon and ends on the one meteorological fact every
commuter already suspects -- a 60% chance the train is a
rumour. Candidate 1's notification-reading wind is funnier
in isolation; candidate 2 is addressed to the judge.
